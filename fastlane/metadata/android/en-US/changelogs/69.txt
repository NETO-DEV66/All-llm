All-LLM 3.9.5 - verify the build, not the agent's word.
- New Verify action in the IDE bar: builds and runs tests; on failure it hands
  the real errors to the agent, applies the fix and retests. Green means exit 0
  was seen, never that the agent claimed success.
- Verification stops instead of spinning: a missing toolchain or an unfixable
  error ends the run with the real reason.
- A banner and the activity feed show each attempt and any applied fix.
