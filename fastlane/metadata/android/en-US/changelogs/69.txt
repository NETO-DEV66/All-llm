All-LLM 3.9.5 - solid surfaces, and a build you can verify.
- Liquid glass is gone for good: panes, sheets and dialogs are opaque again, so
  text never competes with a moving backdrop.
- New Verify action in the IDE bar: builds and runs tests; on failure it hands
  the real errors to the agent, applies the fix, then retests.
- Green means exit 0 was seen, not that the agent claimed success.
