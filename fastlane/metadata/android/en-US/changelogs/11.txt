All-LLM 3.9.1 - cleaner look, navigation drawer, smarter self-repair.
- Removed the liquid-glass look. Panels, cards, bars and sheets are now solid with
  hairline borders and real shadows, which is easier to read and costs no per-frame
  blur, so the app uses less battery while scrolling
- New navigation drawer: the side rail is replaced by a slide-in drawer with a
  hamburger button in every top bar, so screens get their full width back
- Every tap, reveal and transition still bounces, but settles with a single small
  overshoot instead of a hard stop
- Fixed MCP installs for good: installing from Market or Skills now really registers
  the server, custom MCP servers can be added from Skills, and servers installed by
  older builds are repaired on first launch
- The autonomous build-fix loop now retries up to three times instead of once, so a
  fix that uncovers a second compile error can still reach a green build
